and release them publicly upon publication:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the \texttt{provexp} library: sparse provenance matrices, the pullback and pushforward, and coarsening, none
    of which depends on the domain;
  \item the instrumented EMBER v3 feature extractor, which records provenance alongside the original features and
    reproduces them exactly, with tests against a verbatim copy of the reference implementation;
  \item the scripts for every experiment, the perturbation operator with its verification, the planted-trigger
    example, the comparison, and the audit, together with numbered run scripts that reproduce every result in the
    paper from the data, with the command-line arguments and runtimes we used;
  \item the trained models, the explanation-set file lists, the computed explanations, and the result tables.
\end{itemize}
The binaries are not redistributed: they are part of EMBER-2024~\cite{joyce2025ember} and obtainable from its
maintainers under its terms; our scripts identify every file by its hash. The released MalConv checkpoints are those
distributed with EMBER-2024. Reproducing the full evaluation takes about two days on 16 CPU cores and one GPU; the
pilot subset reproduces in hours.
